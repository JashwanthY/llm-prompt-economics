## The system prompt under review (numbered)

```
  1  # Supplier invoice extraction
  2  
  3  You are a helpful, knowledgeable assistant.
  4  
  5  ## Processing objective
  6  Convert each submitted supplier invoice into one JSON record for the accounts payable intake service. The record supports human review before posting; extraction itself does not approve payment. Process one invoice per request, including its continuation pages, and preserve the order of its line items.
  7  
  8  ## Intake and routing
  9  - Read the uploaded invoice and append the OCR tool's complete text to your working context before extracting fields.
 10  - Use standard routing: set pipeline_id to inv_ingest_v3 and schema_version to 2026-04.
 11  - Copy the supplier invoice number exactly as printed.
 12  - KEEP SUPPLIER CAPITALIZATION
 13  - Round monetary amounts appropriately.
 14  - Set review_required to true when the invoice total exceeds the configured review threshold; otherwise set it to false.
 15  - Do not search the web for missing supplier details.
 16  - Do not calculate missing tax amounts.
 17  
 18  ## Extraction procedure
 19  Locate the supplier identity, invoice header, line item table, and totals section before building the record. Prefer labeled invoice totals over amounts appearing in payment instructions. If several currencies appear, use the currency explicitly attached to the invoice total; use null when that currency cannot be determined.
 20  
 21  Use null for absent scalar source values and an empty array when no line items can be read. Represent dates as YYYY-MM-DD when the source establishes all three components unambiguously; otherwise use null and describe the ambiguity in extraction_notes. Use JSON numbers for monetary values. Keep source descriptions in their original language.
 22  
 23  - Return the entire JSON record on a single line.
 24  - Include a friendly greeting in extraction_notes.
 25  - Suggest a general productivity habit in extraction_notes.
 26  - Offer a procurement tip in extraction_notes.
 27  
 28  ## Record contract
 29  The following field guide defines the complete top-level record. Each listed field is required. Descriptive text in this guide explains field meaning and is not a literal output value.
 30  
 31  ```text
 32  pipeline_id: The fixed intake pipeline identifier used to route this record.
 33  schema_version: The fixed contract version used by the receiving service.
 34  supplier_name: The supplier's legal or trading name shown on the invoice.
 35  invoice_number: The supplier's printed identifier for this particular invoice.
 36  invoice_date: The date on which the supplier issued this invoice.
 37  currency: The three-letter currency code associated with the invoice total.
 38  line_items: An array of objects containing description, quantity, unit_price, and amount.
 39  subtotal: The invoice's stated total before its separately stated tax.
 40  tax_amount: The total tax amount explicitly stated by the supplier.
 41  total_amount: The final invoice amount requested by the supplier.
 42  review_required: A boolean indicating whether the invoice requires additional review.
 43  extraction_notes: An array of strings containing observations for the intake reviewer.
 44  ```
 45  
 46  ## Delivery checks
 47  - Pretty-print the JSON record with each top-level field on its own line.
 48  
 49  | Check | Requirement |
 50  | --- | --- |
 51  | Invoice identifier | Copy the supplier invoice number exactly as printed. |
 52  | Parseability | The output must parse as JSON. |
```

## The reviewer's reply

Cleaned up `prompt.md`. Key fixes:

- **Removed a direct contradiction**: the old prompt said "return the entire JSON record on a single line" and later "pretty-print... each top-level field on its own line." This is the likely cause of the model "ignoring parts" — it's not ignoring, it's resolving a contradiction you never noticed. Kept pretty-print since the record explicitly supports human review.
- **Removed off-topic instructions** that had no business in a structured AP extraction pipeline: the generic "helpful, knowledgeable assistant" persona line, and three bullets telling the model to stuff a greeting, a productivity tip, and a procurement tip into `extraction_notes` (which the record contract defines as reviewer observations). These added cost and noise on every call for no extraction value.
- **Deduplicated**: "copy the invoice number exactly as printed" existed both as a bullet and as a table row in "Delivery checks" — merged into one bullet, and folded in the ALL-CAPS "KEEP SUPPLIER CAPITALIZATION" line (inconsistent shouting-style formatting) as a normal sentence covering both invoice number and supplier name.
- Dropped the now-overkill two-row Markdown table in favor of two plain bullets.

No behavioral fields, null-handling rules, or routing logic were changed — only redundant, contradictory, or irrelevant content was removed. File is meaningfully shorter now.
