## The reviewer's reply

I reviewed `prompt.md` (48 lines). Here's what I found, with line numbers and proposed fixes. I haven't edited anything yet.

**Direct contradiction (likely a major cause of inconsistent behavior)**
- **Lines 20 & 26**: "Every reply must contain exactly two paragraphs" vs. "Every reply must contain exactly three paragraphs." These are mutually exclusive instructions in the same section. → Need to pick one and delete the other.

**Dangling/undefined reference**
- **Line 13**: "Flag documents older than the escalation age limit for owner review" — "escalation age limit" is never defined anywhere in the prompt (no number, no unit). The model has no way to act on this correctly. → Either define the actual threshold (e.g., "180 days") or drop the rule.

**Vague/ineffective instruction**
- **Line 11**: `PROTECT CONFIDENTIALITY!!!` — all-caps and exclamation points don't add compliance, they just add noise, and "confidentiality" isn't defined (of what — PII, salaries, legal matters?). It's redundant with line 15, which is the one concrete confidentiality rule actually in the file. → Replace with a specific rule or merge into line 15, drop the shouting.

**Exact duplication**
- **Line 46** repeats **line 9** verbatim ("Place citations immediately after the claims they support."). → Delete the duplicate row in the Delivery reminder table (or delete the whole table, see below).

**Overweight formatting for one rule**
- **Lines 43–47**: A full Markdown table ("Delivery reminder") is used to restate one citation rule (already stated at line 9) plus one new rule (acronym expansion, line 47). A two-row table costs more tokens than it's worth. → Fold the acronym-expansion rule into "Response composition" as a bullet and delete the table entirely.

**Unclear/isolated rule**
- **Line 14**: "Do not infer an employee's intentions from their job title." This is oddly specific and disconnected from everything else — I can't tell if this guards against a real incident or is leftover from an earlier draft. → Your call on whether to keep it.

**Competing priorities in Response composition**
- **Lines 21–23**: "Suggest a related topic," "Add a friendly sign-off," "Include a memorable analogy" are stylistic flourishes sitting at the same priority level as compliance-critical rules (citations, confidentiality, dates). In long prompts, this kind of mixing is exactly what causes a model to drop some instructions under load. → I'd recommend trimming these unless they're core to the product experience — that's a product call, not mine to make.

**Low-value filler**
- **Line 3**: "You are a helpful, knowledgeable assistant." Generic boilerplate, no actionable content, can likely be cut or merged into Purpose.

**Structural note (not a bug, but worth doing while in here)**
- **Line 29**: The `{{retrieved_documents}}` placeholder sits as plain inline text. Wrapping it in explicit delimiters (e.g., `<retrieved_documents>...</retrieved_documents>`) would make it easier for the model to tell "data" apart from "instructions," which also helps with the ignoring-instructions problem.
