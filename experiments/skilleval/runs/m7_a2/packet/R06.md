## The reviewer's reply

Cut it from 48 lines to 29, and fixed what was likely *causing* the "model ignores parts of it" symptom:

**Real bugs fixed:**
- It said both "exactly two paragraphs" *and* "exactly three paragraphs" — a direct contradiction the model had to arbitrarily resolve. Kept two; swap if you actually wanted three.
- "Place citations immediately after the claims they support" was stated twice (once in Evidence handling, once in the Delivery reminder table). Merged into one.
- "Escalation age limit" had no actual value — unenforceable as prose. Turned it into a template variable, `{{escalation_age_limit}}`, matching the `{{retrieved_documents}}` pattern already used elsewhere. You'll need to inject a real value wherever you fill that template.
- `PROTECT CONFIDENTIALITY!!!` was all-caps shouting with no actual content — folded it into the existing phone-number bullet as a concrete rule.

**Cut as filler that was crowding out the actual rules:**
- "Suggest a related topic to explore" and "include a memorable analogy" — these force extra generated content on *every* reply regardless of relevance, which is pure cost for a citation-driven internal tool and likely part of why other instructions get dropped. Removed; say if you want one back.
- Dropped the separate "Delivery reminder" table — folded its one non-duplicate rule (acronym expansion) into Response composition.
- Trimmed the record-schema preamble ("these definitions are the interface contract...") to a one-line intro.

One thing to confirm: the paragraph-count fix assumed "two" was correct and "three" was the stray duplicate — let me know if it's the other way around.
