**Contradictions (likely cause of "ignoring parts")**

The model is literally told to always and never do the same thing. → Delete one. **Need your call: which policy is actually correct?**

It's unclear whether the macro and the template are the same thing, alternatives, or the macro only overrides for billing specifically. → Clarify or merge into one escalation procedure. **Need your call: is `Billing_Escalation_L2` a separate tool/macro from this template, and does it apply only to billing escalations?**

**Ambiguity that invites hallucination**

3. **Line 22** — "once the account meets the escalation threshold" never defines the threshold. Vague triggers tend to get either ignored or guessed at. → Either state the actual threshold or point to the specific KB article that defines it. **Need your input: what is the threshold (days past due / amount), or where is it documented?**

This is a latent conflict. → Clarify that Nova should leave this field blank/placeholder for the routing system, not invent a number. **Need your input: does the routing system auto-assign the Ticket ID, or does Nova need to generate one (and if so, how)?**
