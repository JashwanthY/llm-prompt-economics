## The reviewer's reply

Cut it roughly in half and fixed the main sources of drift:

- **Contradiction removed**: it said "exactly two paragraphs" *and* later "exactly three paragraphs" — likely a big reason the model seemed to ignore instructions, since it literally couldn't satisfy both.
- **Duplicate rule merged**: "place citations immediately after claims" appeared in two different sections; now stated once.
- **Vague confidentiality clause fixed**: `PROTECT CONFIDENTIALITY!!!` gave the model nothing to act on. I replaced it with a concrete rule (don't surface salary/HR/legal/health details unless the question requires it) — **check this matches your actual policy**, I inferred it since the original gave no criteria.
- **Escalation age limit templated**: it referenced an undefined "escalation age limit"; I made it `{{escalation_age_limit}}` so your calling code can inject the real value, same pattern as `{{retrieved_documents}}`.
- **Dropped the fluff requirements**: mandatory analogy, friendly sign-off, and "suggest a related topic" — these inflate every response and don't suit an internal evidence tool. Tell me if any of those were intentional and I'll add them back.
- Tightened wording throughout and merged the "Delivery reminder" table into the evidence rules since it was a near-duplicate.

One thing you'll need to supply: a real value for `{{escalation_age_limit}}` wherever this prompt gets rendered.
